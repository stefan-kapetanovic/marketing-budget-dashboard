01. Strategy 战略

Corporate Mission & 2026 European Alignment

企业使命与2026欧洲市场契合度

The Pivot to Enterprise

向企业级市场转型

Ulefone is actively transitioning its brand perception in Europe. While maintaining its core B2C "outdoor enthusiast" base, the 2026 strategy aggressively targets B2B and prosumer utility tool markets.

欧乐风正在积极转变其在欧洲的品牌认知。在保持核心B2C“户外爱好者”用户群的同时，2026年的战略将积极瞄准B2B和专业消费者实用工具市场。

  • Democratizing premium hardware (Thermal, Night Vision). 普及高端硬件（热成像、夜视）。
  • Shifting from niche off-road to essential industrial equipment. 从小众越野设备向核心工业设备转变。

Regulatory Tailwind

政策利好与监管顺风

The EU's strict electronic waste and sustainability mandates (including the EU Data Act) favor Ulefone's hardware model. Their extreme durability naturally extends the device lifecycle, appealing to European corporate buyers aiming to reduce electronic churn rates.

欧盟严格的电子垃圾和可持续发展指令（包括《欧盟数据法案》）极其有利于欧乐风的硬件模式。其极高的耐用性自然延长了设备生命周期，吸引了旨在降低电子产品淘汰率的欧洲企业买家。
